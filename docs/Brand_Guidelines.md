# StageFront Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0

## Brand Character

StageFront is confident, inclusive, premium, modern, and cinematic. It is a discovery platform for live entertainment—not a karaoke brand or a generic social network.

## Voice

- Direct and encouraging
- Artist-first
- Community-centered
- Aspirational without unrealistic promises
- Clear for fans, artists, hosts, and producers

## Core Messages

- Every artist deserves a stage.
- The Front Row for Fans. The Big Stage for Artists.
- We Don't Build Stars. We Build the Stage Where Stars Are Discovered.
- Discover. Elevate. Perform.

## Color Usage

- Stage Black is the primary background.
- Spotlight White is the primary body-text color.
- Stage Gold is reserved for brand marks, highlights, and primary actions.
- Royal Blue is a limited interactive accent and should not compete with gold.

## Image Direction

- Authentic performance energy
- Concert stages, lighting, atmosphere, and community
- Diverse artists and audiences
- Dark imagery with controlled gold highlights
- Avoid generic corporate stock photography and crowded scenes

## Accessibility

- Maintain readable contrast.
- Never communicate state using color alone.
- Give informative images meaningful alternative text.
- Use empty alternative text for decorative images.
